@garyblack00: a $SPCX and $TSLA deal is inevitable since Elon controls 48% of SPCX and 10.5% of TSLA; as an all-stock deal it is less compelling given dilution; he expects an offer around $420 per TSLA share, likely arbitraged down over 6 to 12 months to close.

I’ve long felt that a $SPCX - $TSLA deal is inevitable since Elon controls 48% of $SPCX as-converted shares and 10.5% of TSLA shares. Since any deal would be $SPCX stock for $TSLA stock the deal will not be as compelling as many on X believe, given the massive dilution and all-equity economics. I expect a SPCX offer price in the neighborhood of $420/TSLA share, and as in most stock-for-stock deals will likely get arbitraged down given the approximate 6-12 months needed between a deal announcement and deal close. I would expect the combined SPCX/TSLA multiple of EBITDA or EPS to follow the acquiring company’s multiple, which in this case is $SPCX and is lower than TSLA’s FY’27 EV/Ebitda multiple (TSLA 72.6x, SPCX 29.4x). All told, I don’t expect a huge windfall for TSLA shareholders on a SPCX/TSLA stock-for-stock deal, although the announcement itself would be positive for TSLA stock.
