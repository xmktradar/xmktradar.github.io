Which accounts does this site track?

It currently tracks 485 accounts: 484 on X and 1 on Truth Social, in 6 categories: major figures and entrepreneurs, macro, equity opinions, fundamentals and themes, semiconductors and AI, and traders. A post is kept only when the post or the post it quotes has a ticker, a company name or ticker from the price data, or a macro term such as the Fed, inflation, jobs, interest rates, or Treasury yields. Chat with none of that is not summarised and is not shown.

How are bullish and bearish decided?

The analyst labels each ticker in a post as one of four stances: bullish, bearish, neutral or no stance. The page reads that per-ticker stance and does not use one stance for the whole post. Only when a post has no per-ticker stance, and the whole post is bullish, bearish or neutral, is that whole-post stance applied to each ticker in the post and marked with a star. Joint bullish means that, inside the day's data window, at least 2 accounts' latest stance on that confirmed US stock is bullish and no account is bearish. Joint bearish is the reverse. If some accounts are bullish and some are bearish, the ticker is a split and is not a joint call. A category marked as excluded from consensus does not take part; all 6 categories currently take part. Neutral and no-stance do not by themselves make a joint call.

How often does the site update?

GitHub Actions rebuilds it automatically every 8 hours. The schedule is 03:00, 11:00 and 19:00 UTC, which is 11:00, 19:00 and 03:00 Hong Kong time.

Is this investment advice?

Not investment advice

No. The site only organises public posts by third parties, for reference. It is not investment advice and it is not an offer to buy or sell any stock.
