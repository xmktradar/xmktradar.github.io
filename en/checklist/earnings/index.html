Full earnings checklist

  1. Before: write down consensus revenue, EPS, YoY; any “must-beat” bar
  2. Before: next-quarter guidance consensus; which line the market fears
  3. Before: check options IV / straddle for the “expected” move size
  4. Before: open Crowd Tape for this ticker’s recent bull/bear and key voices
  5. During: revenue/EPS actual vs estimate; size of beat/miss
  6. During: guidance (revenue/EPS/margins) raised, flat, or cut
  7. During: management tone — defensive, assertive, or dodging demand
  8. During: segment/region mix shifts (don’t stop at the headline)
  9. After: after-hours / next-day price & volume — “good number, bad tape”?
  10. After: compare with Crowd Tape — did the crowd call it early or chase?
  11. After: update your thesis: add, trim, or wait — with conditions
  12. After: if you use options, sanity-check the payoff on OptionStrat (/tools)
