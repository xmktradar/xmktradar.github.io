Embed a sentiment card

Paste this one line into your site, blog or forum post to show the latest count of bullish and bearish accounts on a stock (each account counted once, by its latest view; updated every 4 hours).

<iframe src="https://thecrowdtape.com/embed/NVDA.html" width="320" height="120" loading="lazy"></iframe>

Replace NVDA in the URL with any ticker (upper case), e.g. TSLA or AAPL. The card is 320×120 and contains no JavaScript.

The card summarises public posts only. Not investment advice.
