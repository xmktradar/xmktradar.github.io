Momentum swing trading (Momentum Swing Trading)

The robot mostly trades momentum swings: it only picks the strongest large-cap US stocks, buys the breakout, and holds for days to a few weeks.

Pick strength firstEach month, rank by the past 12-month return (excluding the most recent month), keep the top 10, and require the price to be above the 200-day average
Enter on a breakoutBuy the next session's open when the close is above the prior 20-day high and volume is above the 20-day average
Exit the swingSell at the +2R target, at the stop, or after 20 trading days if neither has hit

Every trade uses the same momentum rules (mom-swing-v1). The details are below.

This page is completely separate from the X-account views above.

Every trade here is chosen by the robot under a fixed rule set, then bought and sold on paper. No human picks the stock, and no real money is used.

The robot that picks, calculates and keeps the book uses Grok, the AI model from xAI, a SpaceX company.

We publish every trade, wins and losses, so you can see whether a rules-only robot can pick stocks.

What the robot does, and what a person does

The robot

  • Ranks the momentum list each month and scans every day for entries
  • Sets stops and targets, enters and exits on paper, keeps the book and measures risk
  • Tags each trade with a technical and a fundamental note (tags only; they do not block an entry)

A person

  • Writes and changes the rules
  • Decides when to use real money (not used now)
  • If real money is used later, a person places the orders

Rules: momentum swing mom-swing-v1 (as of 10/6)

1. 1. What it trades
  • US common stock only, market cap at least $50 billion
  • Long only, cash only, held for days to a few weeks
  • No ETFs (such as QQQ or SPY), no shorting, no margin
  • Planned risk is about 4% of the account per trade (that is 1R), whole shares only. A gap at the open can lose more than 1R
  • One stock can be at most half the account
  • The capital cap is fixed. Only money already booked as profit is added back to available capital
  • With real money later, at most 1 to 3 names at once. During this paper test every signal is taken while cash lasts. If cash is short it is logged as "Skipped, not enough cash" and still tracked (5 names are open on paper now)
2. 2. When it buys
  • The list: each month, the 10 stocks with the strongest 12-month rise (excluding the most recent month), and the price must be above the 200-day average
  • All three must be true at the same day's close:
    1. Close above the prior 20-day high (breakout)
    2. Volume above the 20-day average
    3. Close above the 200-day average
  • If they are, buy the next session's open
  • A new signal in a name already held does not add size
  • If several signal on the same day, they are taken in score order
3. Stop
  • Stop = the lowest price of the 5 days before the signal, minus 0.25 × 14-day ATR (average daily range over 14 days)
  • 1R = entry price minus the stop
  • The stop only moves up, never down. If the open gaps through the stop, sell at the open
4. 4. When it sells
  • Target: sell at +2R
  • If the day's high reaches +1R: move the stop to the entry (breakeven)
  • If the day's high reaches +1.5R: lock the stop at +0.5R
  • Both steps are confirmed after the close and take effect the next session
  • If 20 trading days pass with no target and no stop, sell
  • Dropping out of the top 10 is not a sale. Hold until the exit rules say so
  • A trailing target (a target that follows the price up) is not used. It was to be discussed at the 10/22 review
5. 5. Real money or not

Everything is paper trading. No real money is used. Real money starts only after the test results are in, reviewed, and confirmed by a person. The robot cannot decide to use real money.

6. 6. Rule changes

Rule changes are dated and say what they apply to.

  • 9/24Set mom-swing-v1 (target 3R, hold at most 20 trading days). A trade had to pass technical, fundamental and risk checks before entry
  • 9/30Loosened the fundamental check so only clear problems are rejected. Names marked "watch" enter at half risk. A shadow book records every mechanical signal
  • 10/1The shadow book was merged into the one official paper account. Every mechanical signal enters automatically, and the three checks are tags only. The test was extended to 20 trading days from 9/24 (through the 10/21 close)
  • 10/3Target changed from 3R to 2R (only about 6% of backtest trades reached 3R). The stop became two steps: breakeven at +1R, lock +0.5R at +1.5R, applied immediately to every open position
  • 10/22 (scheduled)Review the 20-day test, then decide whether the rules change

  • Paper account capital: $10,000. The test runs 20 trading days from 2026-09-24, through the 10/21 close
  • Only profit already booked is added to capital (realized profit now: $0.00)
  • Planned risk is about 4% of capital per trade, $400 (that is 1R)
  • Each open trade lists: stock, signal date, entry date, entry price, shares, cost (entry × shares), initial stop, current stop, breakeven trigger (+1R), profit-lock trigger (+1.5R) and locked stop (+0.5R), target (+2R), time-stop date, trading days held, price, and unrealized P&L (dollars, percent, R)
